      Commissioner tools

      Assign a seat pair

      Give an open game and seats to any member. This doesn't use up anyone's turn in the draft order.

      Advance the draft

      Skip 's turn and move to the next member. It uses up their turn but gives them no seats.

      Undo last entry

      Removes the most recent pick, assignment, skip or drop-out, so a skipped or dropped member gets their turn back.

      Drop out of the draft

      You'll keep any seats you've already picked, but you won't get any more turns. If you're on the clock now, this uses your turn.
