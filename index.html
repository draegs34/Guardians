      Assign a seat pair

      Give an open game and seats to any member. This doesn't use up anyone's turn in the draft order.

      Advance the draft

      Skip 's turn and move to the next member. It uses up their turn but gives them nothing.

      Undo last entry

      Removes the most recent pick, assignment, skip or drop-out, so a skipped or dropped member gets their turn back.

      Setup

      Members, PINs, draft order, games, settings, the MLB schedule, a CSV download of picks, and importing data.

      Archive this draft

      Saves every pick in to its own tab in the Google Sheet and clears the board so it's ready for next time. Archived drafts stay viewable under Past drafts.

      Drop out of the draft

      You'll keep anything you've already picked, but you won't get any more turns. If you're on the clock now, this uses your turn.

      Email & push alerts

      Get a message when it's your turn to pick, even when this page is closed. Choose email, push notifications on your phone or computer, or both. These settings cover both drafts.
