    Update prices from Season Ticket Manager

    New price per pair for every open and future seat. Picks already made keep the price they were drafted at.

      Assign a seat pair

      Give an open game and seats to any member. This doesn't use up anyone's turn in the draft order.

      Advance the draft

      Skip 's turn and move to the next member. It uses up their turn but gives them nothing.

      Undo last entry

      Removes the most recent pick, assignment, skip or drop-out, so a skipped or dropped member gets their turn back.

      Archive this draft

      Saves every pick in and clears the board so it's ready for next time. Archived drafts are under Setup → Past drafts for that season type.

      Drop out of the draft

      You'll keep anything you've already picked, but you won't get any more turns. If you're on the clock now, this uses your turn.
